# IMG_0941 — Page 15: Multiplicative seasonal correction & new example

**Content:**

- Continuation of **3.2** (multiplicative model): seasonal coefficients estimated from (n−1) ratios {x_t/m̂_t}, averaged after outlier removal, then proportionally corrected so they sum to p over a period: s*_t = ŝ_t/s̄.
- Definition of the multiplicative **CVS**: x*_t = x_t/s*_t.
- Error calculation: e_t = x_t/(m̂_t × s*_t) = x*_t/m̂_t, i.e., x_t − m̂_t × s*_t.
- **3.3 Exemple de décomposition d'une série chronologique**: introduces a full worked example using aviation fuel sales in France (Tableau 5, quarterly, 2005–2008, in thousands of tonnes, source: Comité Professionnel du Pétrole).
- Determines the period visually: p = 4 (quarterly seasonality).
- Figure 13: the raw quarterly series plotted with the order-4 moving average (M₄(t)) overlaid, 2005–2008.
