# IMG_0944 — Page 18: Prévision (forecasting) intro

**Content:** Start of **Section 4. PRÉVISION**.

- Two forecasting approaches highlighted: (1) parametric trend modeling + seasonal coefficients, (2) exponential smoothing.
- **Notations**: x̂_T(h) := x̂_(T+h), the forecast made at date T for horizon h; hat notation distinguishes estimated (x̂_t) from theoretical (x_t) values.
- Chart: IPCC "cinq scénarios illustratifs" (five emission scenarios), same style as the cover page.
- **Méthode générale de prévision**: a forecast can't be validated on the data used to fit the model — must split into training and test series. Illustrated with a generic train/test timeline diagram and Figure 16 (CAC40 stock index 1992–1998 split into train/test).
- **4.1 Prévision par modélisation de ses composantes tendancielle et saisonnière**: forecast formulas x̂_t = m̂_t + ŝ_t (additive) or x̂_t = m̂_t × ŝ_t (multiplicative), with handwritten notes that m̂_t comes from linear regression on the moving averages (m̂_t = a+bt) and ŝ_t is periodic/known.
- Figure 17: example chart showing x(t), mp(t), m^(t) and the fitted forecast model x^(t) overlaid.
