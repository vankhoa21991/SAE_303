# IMG_0938 — Page 12: La droite de tendance (trend line)

**Content:** **2.2 La droite de tendance**.

- When the moving-average series shows a linear trend, a regression line D_(m_p,t)/t is fit to it: y = β₁x + β₀, with β₁ = Cov(m_p,t, t)/Var(t) and β₀ = m̄_p,t − β₁·t̄ (formulas completed by hand).
- Figure 9: example series (x_t) with its moving average (mp(t)) and the fitted linear trend line "Linéaire(x(t)) = 0.2789t + 3.578" plotted over 12 time points.
- Remark: a cruder alternative would be fitting the trend directly to x_t, but |ρ_x,t| ≤ |ρ_mp,t| (seasonal variation lowers the correlation for the raw series); handwritten reminder that there's no more seasonal variation once you're working with m_p,t.
- Conclusion: a linear model isn't fully satisfactory (many points deviate) but serves as a first approximation before incorporating seasonal variations.
