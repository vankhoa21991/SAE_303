**Content:**

- Figure 7: "Influence de l'ordre d'une moyenne mobile" — oyster sales (Tableau: quarterly tonnes 2010–2013) plotted with moving averages of order 3 and 4; handwritten annotations note order 4 removes the period-4 seasonality ("saisonnalité de période 4").
- Note: if the moving-average series shows a clear linear trend, a regression line can be fit to it.
- **2.1.2 Limitations et alternatives**:
  - **Limitations**: a change in trend level/slope causes a poor approximation around that date (Figure 8 illustrates this lag/distortion effect on a sample series).
  - **Effet de Slutsky-Yule**: smoothing can introduce spurious correlated oscillations not present in the original data; mentions ACF/PACF as tools (not covered in this course).
  - **Extension du concept**: weighted moving averages (e.g., Gaussian moving average) can give more weight to central or to more recent observations (handwritten note).
  - **Médiane mobile simple**: same technique but using the median instead of the mean — less sensitive to outliers.
